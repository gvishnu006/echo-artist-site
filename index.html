03 / About

Made for the
in-between hours.

ECHO is the solo project of producer and multi-instrumentalist Eli Novak, built from tape loops, broken synths and field recordings captured between Berlin and Lisbon.

Since 2022 the project has moved from bedroom demos to festival stages, collecting a devoted following for its widescreen sound and intimate, strobe-lit live shows.
